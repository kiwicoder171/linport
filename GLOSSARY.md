# Lin's Art Museum

A browser-based, spatial 3D art museum exhibition that showcases the artwork, character designs, and sketches of visual artist Lin.

## Language

**Museum**:
The overall spatial 3D environment housing all exhibition halls, entrances, and curatorial zones.
_Avoid_: Website, web app

**Exhibition Hall**:
A dedicated architectural room or wing within the museum focused on a specific body of work (e.g. Masterworks, Sketches).
_Avoid_: Page, tab, section

**Masterwork**:
A fully rendered, high-resolution finished digital illustration mounted on a feature gallery wall.
_Avoid_: PNG, image file, hero asset

**Study**:
An exploratory sketch, rough concept, or pencil/ink drawing displayed in the atelier or archive wing.
_Avoid_: Thumbnail, draft, scrap

**Placard**:
An architectural label mounted beside or beneath an artwork displaying its title, year, medium, and curatorial statement.
_Avoid_: Tooltip, caption, badge

**Catalog Index**:
The formal archival identifier (e.g. "Masterwork #01", "Study #04") assigned to an untitled artwork until a custom title is designated by the artist.
_Avoid_: File name, image ID, slug

**Inspection View**:
A focused interaction mode where the camera glides close to a selected artwork, dimming ambient room light to reveal high-detail brushwork and notes.
_Avoid_: Modal, popup, lightbox

**Walkthrough**:
The guided, scroll-driven camera journey that navigates visitors seamlessly through exhibition halls.
_Avoid_: Scrolling page, slider, carousel

**Foyer**:
The opening architectural space of the museum introducing the artist, curatorial statement, and exhibition title before visitors enter the galleries.
_Avoid_: Hero section, banner, splash screen

**Atelier**:
Hall II of the museum, designed as an intimate studio space showcasing sketches, process studies, and exploratory works on drafting tables and pinboards.
_Avoid_: Archive, secondary page, scrap pile

**Centerpiece**:
A procedural 3D sculpture or physical architectural installation positioned at a gallery focal point to provide spatial depth and anchor the room's geometry.
_Avoid_: Prop, 3D model, dummy mesh

**Curatorial Desk**:
The concluding area of the walkthrough providing artist biography, commission availability, and direct contact avenues.
_Avoid_: Footer, contact form
