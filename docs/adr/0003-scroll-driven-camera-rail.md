# 0003: Scroll-Driven Camera Rail Navigation

We decided to use a choreographed scroll-driven spline camera rail as the primary navigation model, paired with click-to-inspect focal zooming, rather than first-person WASD / free-roam controls. WASD controls have high friction on mobile devices, cause disorienting collisions with 3D geometry, and fail to guarantee aesthetic composition. A scroll-guided path ensures every artwork is framed from its optimal curatorial vantage point.
